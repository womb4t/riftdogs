# Riftdogs terms of play

These terms apply when you play Riftdogs online, on the community server or any server you were invited to. Offline play is yours alone.

1. **One skipper per person.** Your invite or sign-up is for you. Don't share your login or invite code; anyone holding it can play as you.
2. **Play fair.** The galaxy, its markets and its news are shared by every skipper. Don't cheat, use exploits, edit saves, automate play or attack the server.
3. **Credits gained by cheating, exploits or editing saves may be removed without notice.**
4. **Accounts can be closed.** The operator may block or delete an account that breaks these terms or harms other players' game.
5. **No real money.** Credits, cargo, ships and payment codes have no value outside the game and can't be sold or exchanged for money.
6. **No guarantees.** Riftdogs is in testing. The server can be down, reset or changed, and progress can be lost. Keep playing offline whenever you like.
7. **Your data.** What the server keeps about you, and how to delete it, is in PRIVACY.md.

These terms may change as the game grows; the game folder always has the current version.
