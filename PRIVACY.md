# Riftdogs privacy notice

Offline play keeps your game and your save on your own computer. Every copy of the game, online or offline, does tell Riftdogs that it exists; the next section says exactly what is sent.

## Every copy of the game

Whenever the game is installed and your computer is online, it checks in with Riftdogs, offline play included. It sends:

- **Your email.** The game asks for it the first time you start. It is used for your place in the online queue, replies to your bug reports and essential notices about the game, and the welcome and reminder emails when you are let in. **News and offers are sent only if you tick the box for them.** Never sold, and shared only with Resend to deliver these emails (see "Emails when you're let in" below).
- **Proof that it is this install.** The first time it checks in, the Riftdogs server gives this computer an **install ID** (a random number) and a **secret install key**, which the game keeps in a private file here. The server keeps only a scrambled form of the key, which can't be turned back into it.
- **The game version** and **your computer's platform** (for example linux or darwin). Nothing else about your computer.
- **Bug reports and suggestions** you log in the game, when this computer is next online. They carry your text, the game version, platform, screen and star system, and no save file.
- **Your Riftdogs login, if this computer has one**, so the server can link this install to your commander: then your place in the queue counts as taken and your bug reports show your commander name.

Riftdogs sends back your place in the queue, whether an online place is ready for you, and whether an update is needed or recommended. It can also send **messages** to your copy of the game. They appear on the start screen under FROM RIFTDOGS and in the communicator log; the game keeps them on this computer, with which ones you have read. **Each message is deleted from the server once it expires, at most 90 days after it is sent.**

- **An install not seen for 24 months is deleted**, with its messages, and its email once no other copy of the game uses that email.
- **Forget this computer:** Connection settings → Forget this computer (if you have an online account it reads Remove my email, which does the same for your email). Riftdogs deletes this install and the messages sent to it, and, once no other copy of the game uses your email, your email, your place in the queue and the messages sent to that email. The game forgets its install ID and key here. Bug reports already sent are kept without the install ID. You can give an email again later to be checked in again.

Playing offline never sends your save, your pilot or what you do in the game.

## Your IP address

Whenever the game or riftdogs.com connects to the Riftdogs server, the server sees your computer's internet (IP) address. It uses it only in memory, to limit how many requests one address can make, so nobody can flood the server or guess codes. It is not written to disk or logged.

When you play online, the server you joined keeps the following.

## What is stored, and why

- **Your commander name and pilot number**, so other pilots can see you and the server knows whose save is whose. See "Your commander name" below.
- **Your private login**, kept on the server only in a scrambled form that can't be turned back into the login. The login itself stays on your computer.
- **Your email, if you gave one**, and when you gave it. If you joined the galaxy from the queue, it is the email your copy of the game checked in with. It is used for the things listed under "Every copy of the game" above, and for Riftdogs news only if you asked for news: never sold, shared only with Resend to deliver these emails, and never used for anything else.
- **Your saves** and a short history of recent ones, so you can play from any computer and recover from a lost or broken save.
- **Trade records**: what you bought and sold, where and when, at what price, and payment codes you made or redeemed. They keep the shared markets working and fair.
- **Feedback**: the bug reports and suggestions you send from the game, with your commander name, game version, computer platform, the screen you were on and your star system, so they can be fixed.
- **Automatic checks**: the server checks saves and trades against what it saw happen, to keep the shared economy fair, and keeps what those checks find with your account.
- **Market traffic**: the shared markets remember which ships visited and traded where, recently. These traces fade over time.

## The communicator

Online, the communicator keeps **your ship number**, **your contacts and contact requests**, **the pilots you block**, **the messages you send and receive** (direct messages and channel 16 calls), and **reports** you make about a message. This is kept so messages can reach you, including while you are offline, and so abuse can be dealt with.

- **Messages, contact requests and reports are deleted automatically after 30 days.** Your contacts and the pilots you block stay until you delete your account.
- **A message you report is read by the operator of the server**, together with who reported it and who sent it. Other pilots cannot read your direct messages. The operator can reach the server's data, but only looks at messages that are reported or needed to deal with abuse.
- Blocking is private: the pilot you block is not told.
- **Deleting your account deletes all of it** (contacts, blocks, messages and reports). Your ship number is retired and never used again.

## Your commander name

Your pilot number is your identity in the game. The server keeps **your commander name, and every name you have had**, with **when each began and how**: chosen, renamed or reset. Other pilots in the game see your current name and pilot number, and your former names with their dates. Your private login stays on your computer. An older account whose commander name was taken from its account name shows that name.

If another pilot reports your name, the operator of the server sees the reported name, **who reported it**, and who it belongs to. Nothing else is shared with the reporter.

**Deleting your account deletes all of it:** your names and their history, and the reports you made. Your pilot number is retired and never used again.

## Your Trade Dog Licence

If you're issued a Trade Dog Licence, the server keeps **its number** (your ship number), **when it was issued** and **which parts of it you've made public** (your skills and your check flight), so your licence can be shown the way you chose. The card always shows your commander name. Your skills and check-flight results themselves are in your save. Today nothing from your licence is published anywhere: the QR code on the card leads to riftdogs.com, and pilot profile pages don't exist yet. When they do, they will show only what you made public. Deleting your account deletes your licence.

## riftdogs.com

The website sets **no cookies** and uses **no analytics or tracking**. Its pages, fonts, pictures and video all come from riftdogs.com itself. The one exception is Cloudflare's spam check for the forms (below), which loads from Cloudflare on the home page. The only things it sends to the Riftdogs server are the forms you fill in (below) and a request for the Relay's latest headlines.

**The forms are checked by Cloudflare Turnstile**, to stop spam robots. The waiting list, the news sign-up and the bug report form each show Cloudflare's check, which loads from Cloudflare. To tell a person from a robot it looks at your browser and how it behaves, and Cloudflare receives your IP address when the home page loads the check, whether or not you use a form. Cloudflare says it does not use this for advertising or to track you across sites, and handles this data under its own [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The Riftdogs server sends Cloudflare only the check's one-time answer, never your email, what you wrote, or your IP address.

## The waiting list

If you join the waiting list on riftdogs.com, we keep **your email address and when you joined**, **which computer you'll play on**, **whether you've tested games before and, if you have, what you wrote about it**, and **your referral code and who referred you** (whose link you joined through, if anyone's). We use your answers only to choose testers for each batch, and your email only to email you when there's a place for you, and with occasional Riftdogs news: never sold, shared only with Resend to deliver these emails, and never used for anything else.

Your referral code makes your personal invite link. When a friend who wasn't already known to Riftdogs joins through it and then installs the game with that same email, we record that the referral counted, and the queue moves you both up (up to five friends each). So that each friend counts only once, the record keeps a scrambled form of your friend's email, made with a secret key kept on the Riftdogs server, which can't be turned back into the address; it is kept for up to 24 months, even if your friend leaves the list. The queue order guides us, but we choose each batch ourselves.

To leave the list, email hello@riftdogs.com from that address: your email, answers, referral code and email records are deleted. A referral already counted stays counted for whoever referred you. Waiting-list addresses and referral records are deleted after 24 months.

If you send a bug report or suggestion from riftdogs.com, we keep **what you wrote, whether it's a bug or a suggestion, and when you sent it**, plus **your email address if you gave one**. We use them only to fix the game and, if you gave an email, to reply to you. To have a report deleted, email hello@riftdogs.com.

## Emails when you're let in

When we let you in (from the waiting list or the game's queue), Riftdogs emails the address you joined with. These are not news, so you get them whether or not you ticked the news box.

- **A welcome email**, when you are let in. It says how to get the game.
- **Reminders**, only if the welcome email was sent: one after 3 days and one after 10 days if you haven't installed the game; one 2 days after you install it if you have never flown; and one after 14 days without playing. After that last one, nothing more comes until you play again and then stop for 14 days again.
- **Every email has a "Stop these emails" link.** Open it and press the button, and no more of these emails are sent. The link contains no email address. So that a returning address stays opted out, we keep only a scrambled form of your address, which can't be turned back into it. It is kept even when your other records are deleted. You can also reply to any of these emails, or write to hello@riftdogs.com, and ask us to stop.

The emails are sent by **Resend**, an email delivery company. It receives your address and the email, so it can deliver it. Resend keeps its own delivery record of each email (the address, the email and whether it arrived) under its own rules, and deleting your records here doesn't delete that copy. We use no tracking pixels and no click tracking, so we do not know whether you opened an email or clicked a link. Replies go to hello@riftdogs.com.

We keep queued and sent email records (your address, which email it was, and when it was queued or sent) so each email goes out once. **They are deleted when you delete your account, use Remove my email, or leave the waiting list, and after 24 months at the latest.**

## How long it is kept

- Your account, saves and records are kept while your account exists.
- **Communicator messages, contact requests and reports are deleted after 30 days.** Contacts and blocks are kept while your account exists.
- Save history is pruned to the most recent saves as you play.
- **Emails of accounts unused for 24 months are deleted** automatically, with the email records described above.
- **The opt-out record** (the scrambled form of an address that opted out of the emails) is kept until you ask us to remove it, so you are not emailed again.
- Backups are kept for up to 30 days, so deleted data leaves them within 30 days.

## How to delete it

- **Remove my email:** Connection settings → Remove my email. The server forgets your email and when you gave it, and takes it off the waiting list; you get no more news and no more of the emails above. The game then forgets this computer as Forget this computer does, and asks for an email again before the next game.
- **Delete my account:** Connection settings → Delete my account, then type your pilot number to confirm. The server deletes your account, login, email, saves, save history, trade records, communicator data, commander names, licence and messages waiting for you at once, and from its backups within 30 days. The game forgets your login and its copy of your online save on this computer and, for an account on the Riftdogs server, forgets this computer's install as Forget this computer does; your email's place in the queue goes too, and the email itself once no other copy uses it. Payment codes you made that nobody has redeemed yet are cancelled. Records other players need (a payment code they redeemed from you) and your bug reports are kept without your name, though another player's own statement still shows the line it got (for example a payment from you).
- **Or ask the operator** of the server you play on, who can do the same for you.

Your offline pilot is never touched by either.
