# Riftdogs privacy notice

Offline play keeps your game and your save on your own computer. Every copy of the game, online or offline, does tell Riftdogs that it exists; the next section says exactly what is sent.

## Every copy of the game

Whenever the game is installed and your computer is online, it checks in with Riftdogs, offline play included. It sends:

- **Your email.** The game asks for it the first time you start. It is used for your place in the online queue, replies to your bug reports and essential notices about the game. **News and offers are sent only if you tick the box for them.** Never sold or shared.
- **Proof that it is this install.** The first time it checks in, the Riftdogs server gives this computer an **install ID** (a random number) and a **secret install key**, which the game keeps in a private file here. The server keeps only a scrambled form of the key, which can't be turned back into it.
- **The game version** and **your computer's platform** (for example linux or darwin). Nothing else about your computer.
- **Bug reports and suggestions** you log in the game, when this computer is next online. They carry your text, the game version, platform, screen and star system, and no save file.
- **Your Riftdogs login, if this computer has one**, so the server can link this install to your commander: then your place in the queue counts as taken and your bug reports show your commander name.

Riftdogs sends back your place in the queue, whether an online place is ready for you, and whether an update is needed or recommended. It can also send **messages** to your copy of the game. They appear on the start screen under FROM RIFTDOGS and in the communicator log; the game keeps them on this computer, with which ones you have read. **Each message is deleted from the server once it expires, at most 90 days after it is sent.**

- **An install not seen for 24 months is deleted**, with its messages, and its email once no other copy of the game uses that email.
- **Forget this computer:** Connection settings → Forget this computer (if you have an online account it reads Remove my email, which does the same for your email). Riftdogs deletes this install and the messages sent to it, and, once no other copy of the game uses your email, your email, your place in the queue and the messages sent to that email. The game forgets its install ID and key here. Bug reports already sent are kept without the install ID. You can give an email again later to be checked in again.

Playing offline never sends your save, your pilot or what you do in the game.

When you play online, the server you joined keeps the following.

## What is stored, and why

- **Your commander name**, so other pilots can see you and the server knows whose save is whose.
- **Your private login**, kept on the server only in a scrambled form that can't be turned back into the login. The login itself stays on your computer.
- **Your email, if you gave one**, and when you gave it. If you joined the galaxy from the queue, it is the email your copy of the game checked in with. It is used for the things listed under "Every copy of the game" above, and for Riftdogs news only if you asked for news: never sold, shared or used for anything else.
- **Your saves** and a short history of recent ones, so you can play from any computer and recover from a lost or broken save.
- **Trade records**: what you bought and sold, where and when, at what price, and payment codes you made or redeemed. They keep the shared markets working and fair.
- **Feedback**: the bug reports and suggestions you send from the game, with your commander name, game version, computer platform, the screen you were on and your star system, so they can be fixed.
- **Automatic checks**: the server checks saves and trades against what it saw happen, to keep the shared economy fair, and keeps what those checks find with your account.
- **Market traffic**: the shared markets remember which ships visited and traded where, recently. These traces fade over time.

## The communicator

Online, the communicator keeps **your ship number**, **your contacts and contact requests**, **the pilots you block**, **the messages you send and receive** (direct messages and channel 16 calls), and **reports** you make about a message. This is kept so messages can reach you, including while you are offline, and so abuse can be dealt with.

- **Messages, contact requests and reports are deleted automatically after 30 days.** Your contacts and the pilots you block stay until you delete your account.
- **A message you report is read by the operator of the server**, together with who reported it and who sent it. Other pilots cannot read your direct messages. The operator can reach the server's data, but only looks at messages that are reported or needed to deal with abuse.
- Blocking is private: the pilot you block is not told.
- **Deleting your account deletes all of it** (contacts, blocks, messages and reports). Your ship number is retired and never used again.

## The waiting list

If you join the waiting list on riftdogs.com, we keep **your email address and when you joined**, and nothing else. We use it only to email you when there's a place for you, and with occasional Riftdogs news: never sold, shared or used for anything else. To leave the list, email hello@riftdogs.com from that address. Waiting-list addresses are deleted after 24 months.

If you send a bug report or suggestion from riftdogs.com, we keep **what you wrote, whether it's a bug or a suggestion, and when you sent it**, plus **your email address if you gave one**. We use them only to fix the game and, if you gave an email, to reply to you. To have a report deleted, email hello@riftdogs.com.

## How long it is kept

- Your account, saves and records are kept while your account exists.
- **Communicator messages, contact requests and reports are deleted after 30 days.** Contacts and blocks are kept while your account exists.
- Save history is pruned to the most recent saves as you play.
- **Emails of accounts unused for 24 months are deleted** automatically.
- Backups are kept for up to 30 days, so deleted data leaves them within 30 days.

## How to delete it

- **Remove my email:** Connection settings → Remove my email. The server forgets your email and when you gave it; you get no more news. The game then forgets this computer as Forget this computer does, and asks for an email again before the next game.
- **Delete my account:** Connection settings → Delete my account, then type your commander name to confirm. The server deletes your account, login, email, saves, save history, trade records, communicator data and messages waiting for you at once, and from its backups within 30 days. The game forgets your login and its copy of your online save on this computer and, for an account on the Riftdogs server, forgets this computer's install as Forget this computer does; your email's place in the queue goes too, and the email itself once no other copy uses it. Payment codes you made that nobody has redeemed yet are cancelled. Records other players need (a payment code they redeemed from you) and your bug reports are kept without your name, though another player's own statement still shows the line it got (for example a payment from you).
- **Or ask the operator** of the server you play on, who can do the same for you.

Your offline pilot is never touched by either.
